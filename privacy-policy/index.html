1. Introduction

Cloud Lab (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides the Reminders CL mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and store it, and your choices regarding your information when you use the App.

Reminders CL is a reminder and event organization tool. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice. Any checkup or medical appointment information you store in the App is for your personal organization only.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the App, and limited technical information needed to operate and secure the App. We do not collect advertising identifiers or sell your data.

3. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A password you create (stored in an encrypted form by our authentication provider)
  • Profile information you choose to add, such as your name

Authentication is provided by Supabase using email and password.

4. User-Created Content

You can create, edit, and delete reminders, events, anniversaries, checkup dates, and related notes. This content is associated with your account and stored in our database. You retain ownership of the content you create, and we only use it to provide the App’s features to you.

5. Group and Shared Information

The App allows you to create or join groups and share events with group members. Each group has roles: OWNER, ADMIN, and MEMBER. Group information, including group names, member lists, and shared events, is stored so that group members can view and interact with shared content according to their role and permissions.

By joining or creating a group, you choose to share relevant information with other members of that group.

6. Reminder and Event Information

Reminders CL stores the dates, titles, descriptions, and recurrence settings for reminders and events you create. This includes anniversaries and medical or checkup appointment reminders. We treat this information as personal organization data and do not analyze it for purposes beyond delivering reminders and organizing your schedule.

7. Device and Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the App, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and error logs. This information helps us maintain performance and fix issues. We do not track your precise location.

8. Notifications

Reminders CL uses local notifications on your device to alert you about upcoming events and reminders. Notification content is generated locally from the data you have stored. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

9. How Information Is Used

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Display your reminders, events, and group-shared content
  • Send local notifications for reminders
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Monitor and address technical issues

We do not use your information to show advertisements.

10. How Information Is Stored

Your account data and user-created content are stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by authentication and access controls.

11. Supabase as a Service Provider

We use Supabase for authentication and database services. Supabase processes and stores data on our behalf. Your use of the App is also subject to Supabase’s terms and privacy practices, which govern how Supabase handles infrastructure data. We do not control Supabase’s infrastructure, but we configure our project to restrict access to authorized users.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following situations:

  • With Supabase, our infrastructure and authentication provider
  • With service providers who assist us in operating the App and who are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

13. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. If you delete your account or specific content, we will remove it from our active database within a reasonable time, except where we are required to retain information by law.

14. Account and Data Deletion

You can create, edit, and delete your own data according to your permissions. You may also request account deletion by contacting us at the email address below. When your account is deleted, your personal data and user-created content will be removed from our active systems, subject to any legal retention requirements.

15. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including using encrypted connections, access controls, and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Children’s Privacy

Reminders CL is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.

17. International Data Processing

Supabase and our infrastructure providers may process and store data in various countries. By using the App, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in locations outside your country of residence, subject to the safeguards described in this policy and Supabase’s terms.

18. User Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can manage much of your data directly in the App. For other requests, contact us using the information below.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by updating the effective date. Your continued use of the App after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
