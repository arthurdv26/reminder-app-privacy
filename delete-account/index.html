Information to include in your request

Please include the following in your deletion request:

  • The email address associated with your Reminders CL account
  • A clear request to delete the account
  • Any additional information needed to identify the account

Do not include your password or any authentication credentials.

Data that will be deleted

Subject to applicable legal or operational requirements, account-associated data that may be deleted includes:

  • Your Supabase authentication account
  • Your user profile
  • Group membership data associated with your account
  • Events created by you
  • Reminder data associated with your account
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Security
  • Fraud prevention
  • Dispute resolution
  • Legitimate operational requirements

Processing

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
